Frequently Asked Questions

1. How soon after hospital discharge should rehabilitation begin?

Rehabilitation can begin as soon as the treating surgeon or neurologist provides clinical clearance, usually within 24 to 48 hours of returning home.

2. Can CPM machines be rented for home use?

Yes, our equipment division Ashok Surgical delivers calibrated knee Continuous Passive Motion (CPM) machines directly to your bedside.

3. Does your therapist communicate with our surgeon?

Yes, we chart daily joint range of motion (ROM) and share clinical progress reports directly with your operating physician.
