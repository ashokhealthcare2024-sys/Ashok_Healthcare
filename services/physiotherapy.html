Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential answers regarding home physical therapy sessions, clinical qualifications, protocols, and scheduling.

How long is each home physiotherapy session?

Each standard home therapy session lasts between 45 to 60 minutes, depending on the patient's condition, tolerance, and clinical requirements.

Do I need a doctor's prescription to start home physiotherapy?

While a doctor’s prescription is helpful (especially for post-surgical protocol alignment), you can also book a direct consultation with our senior physiotherapist for general joint pain, back strain, or age-related mobility issues.

Are your home physiotherapists certified?

Yes. All physiotherapists at Ashok Healthcare hold recognized Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) or Master of Physiotherapy (MPT) degrees with clinical experience in hospital and home-care environments.

How many sessions will I need to recover?

The number of sessions depends on the severity of the condition. Acute back or neck sprains may require 5 to 7 sessions, while major surgical recoveries (such as TKR or ACL reconstruction) typically follow 14-day or 30-day care plans.

What happens if my assigned therapist is unable to attend a session?

Under our Substitution Guarantee, if your primary therapist faces an emergency or scheduled leave, we dispatch a qualified replacement therapist who reviews your clinical case notes to ensure uninterrupted recovery.
