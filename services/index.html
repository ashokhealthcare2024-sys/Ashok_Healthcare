How We Help at Home

Home Healthcare Services

1. Professional Home Nursing Care in Bangalore

Nursing support for post-operative and chronic care at home, based on the treating clinician’s plan.

  • Surgical wound dressing, drain observation, and infection-control practices.
  • IV care, injections, catheter care, and Ryle’s Tube/NG feeding as prescribed and within scope.
  • Tracheostomy suctioning, PEG feeding support, and palliative nursing as directed.
  • 12-hour day/night shifts and 24-hour live-in arrangements, subject to availability.

2. Emergency Home ICU Setup & Critical Care

Coordinate selected home ICU equipment and bedside nursing after clinical assessment.

  • Portable ventilators, multipara monitors, suction units, and syringe or infusion pumps.
  • One-to-one nursing where appropriate and available.
  • Home setup and equipment use coordinated with the treating clinical team.
  • Availability of power backup, remote monitoring, and technical response should be confirmed for each setup.

3. Elderly Care & Assisted Living at Home

Daily assistance designed to help seniors stay safe and comfortable at home.

  • Bathing, grooming, dressing, oral hygiene, and mobility assistance.
  • Reminders for prescribed medicines and routine observations as agreed.
  • Companionship and daily engagement for people with memory or cognitive conditions.
  • Meal and feeding support aligned with family instructions and prescribed diet.

4. Neuro & Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Home physiotherapy and rehabilitation support based on individual assessment.

  • Stroke and paralysis recovery exercises and mobility practice.
  • Rehabilitation after knee or hip replacement and fracture stabilization.
  • Gait, balance, posture, and fall-prevention training.
  • Speech or other specialist therapy referrals can be discussed with the treating team.

5. Medical Equipment Rental & Sales

Ashok Surgical coordinates available, sanitized home-care equipment; confirm clinical suitability and rental terms before booking.

  • 5L and 10L oxygen concentrators and cylinders, subject to availability.
  • 3-function and 5-function motorized hospital beds and pressure-relief mattresses.
  • BiPAP/CPAP, portable suction units, and nebulizers.
  • Delivery and setup timing varies by location and inventory.

6. 24/7 Emergency Ambulance Service

ALS ambulance coordination for inter-hospital transfers and urgent transport across Greater Bangalore. Equipment, crew, and dispatch timing depend on availability. For an immediate life-threatening emergency, contact local emergency services.

Services FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which areas in Bangalore do you cover for home healthcare and equipment delivery?

Our operations hub is in Yeshwanthpur. We coordinate services across Yeshwanthpur, Rajajinagar, Malleshwaram, Hebbal, Mathikere, Peenya, Jalahalli, Basaveshwar Nagar, Mahalakshmi Layout, Sadashivanagar, Vidyaranyapura, Yelahanka, and other Greater Bengaluru areas, subject to availability.

2. How quickly can you set up a ventilator or Home ICU?

We target 3 to 4 hours for eligible requests in many North Bengaluru areas, after assessment and confirmation of equipment and staff availability. Please call to confirm the expected timing for your location.

3. Can we rent medical equipment like oxygen concentrators or ICU beds on a weekly basis?

Daily, weekly, and monthly rental arrangements may be available for equipment such as oxygen concentrators, hospital beds, and monitors. Confirm price, availability, rental terms, and clinical suitability with Ashok Surgical.

4. Are your home nurses qualified to manage tracheostomy care and catheterization?

Qualified GNM and B.Sc Nursing professionals may perform procedures prescribed by the treating clinician and within their training and scope of practice, including tracheostomy suctioning, catheter care, IV line care, and tube-feeding support.

5. How do you handle medical equipment breakdowns during late hours?

Contact technical support for troubleshooting guidance and service coordination. Technician dispatch and replacement equipment depend on the location and availability. If a patient's condition is urgent, call emergency services and follow the treating clinician’s instructions.
