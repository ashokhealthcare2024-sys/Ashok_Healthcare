Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about our ambulance fleet, booking procedures, and emergency transport capabilities.

What is the difference between a BLS and an ALS ambulance?

A Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulance is suitable for non-critical patients who require basic stretchers, wheelchairs, and oxygen en route. An Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulance acts as a mobile ICU with a ventilator, cardiac monitor, defibrillator, and trained paramedic for critical care.

How do I book an emergency ambulance with Ashok Healthcare?

Call our dedicated 24/7 hotline at +91 78297 53538. Share your exact pickup location, patient condition, and destination hospital for immediate dispatch.

Can I pre-book an ambulance for a scheduled hospital discharge or appointment?

Yes. You can schedule non-emergency patient transfers hours or days in advance to guarantee timely pickup and comfortable transit for hospital visits or discharges.

Do you provide long-distance outstation ambulance transfers from Bangalore?

Yes. We provide long-distance inter-city and inter-state medical transport with continuous medical monitoring, oxygen cylinders, and rotating drivers for safe transit.

Is an EMT/Paramedic present in the ambulance during transport?

Yes. All ALS emergency ambulances include a trained paramedic or ICU nurse, and BLS ambulances include an emergency medical technician (EMT) or driver trained in basic first aid and patient handling.
