Sample Collection & Integrity Process

01 · Collection

A trained collection professional uses appropriate single-use collection supplies and follows applicable hygiene practices.

02 · Sample handling

Samples are labelled and transported under handling conditions appropriate to the test and partner laboratory instructions.

03 · Laboratory processing

The sample is sent to the processing laboratory selected for the test; ask the team about that laboratory's accreditation scope.

04 · Results

Report availability and delivery method depend on the test and laboratory; the expected turnaround is confirmed when booking.

  1. Collection staff: Collection appointments are assigned according to location, test requirements, and staff availability.
  2. Cold-chain handling: Temperature-sensitive samples are handled and transported according to the laboratory's requirements.
  3. Partner testing: Laboratory name and relevant accreditation details can be confirmed for the tests in your booking.
  4. Digital reports: Where supported by the partner laboratory, reports may be shared through available digital channels. Turnaround varies, particularly for cultures and specialized testing.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I prepare for a fasting blood test at home?

Preparation depends on the test and your clinician's instructions. Follow the instructions from your doctor or laboratory; do not fast or change medicines unless instructed. Ask the booking team to confirm preparation and collection time.

2. How will I receive my diagnostic test reports?

Report delivery method and timing depend on the test and partner laboratory. The team can confirm the expected turnaround and available delivery options when your booking is placed.

3. Can I get an ECG done at home?

Home ECG appointments may be available, subject to location and scheduling. Confirm the service and report-review arrangements when booking. If you have urgent or severe symptoms, seek emergency medical care rather than waiting for a home appointment.

4. Are there extra home collection charges?

Collection charges, minimum order values, and package offers depend on the tests, location, and current booking terms. Confirm the total payable amount before booking.

5. Are your diagnostic partner laboratories accredited?

Accreditation and scope depend on the partner processing a particular test. Ask for the partner laboratory and its applicable accreditation details for the tests in your booking.
