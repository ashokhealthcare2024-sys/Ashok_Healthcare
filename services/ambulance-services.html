Emergency Dispatch & Transfer Coordination

01 · Call & location

02 · Dispatch assessment

The team reviews the transfer request and available vehicle, crew, and equipment options.

03 · En-route care

Care during transit depends on the dispatched unit, accompanying staff, and patient's needs.

04 · Destination handover

Share relevant patient information and handover details with the receiving facility or caregiver.

  1. Call triage: Describe the patient's condition and location so dispatch can understand the request.
  2. Vehicle selection: ALS/BLS availability and suitability are discussed based on the information provided and crew assessment.
  3. Transit coordination: Follow the dispatch team's instructions. Do not delay emergency care while arranging a private transfer.
  4. Hospital handover: Keep prescriptions, discharge notes, and relevant medical information ready where possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between a BLS and an ALS ambulance?

BLS transport is generally for patients needing basic transport support, while ALS units may provide advanced monitoring and life-support equipment with qualified clinical crew. The right vehicle depends on patient needs and availability; ask dispatch to help assess your request.

2. How do I book an emergency ambulance with Ashok Healthcare?

Call +91 78297 53538 and share the pickup location, destination, callback number, and a brief description of the patient's needs. Dispatch will confirm available vehicle/crew options and an estimated arrival. For immediate life-threatening emergencies, contact local emergency services.

3. Can I pre-book an ambulance for a scheduled hospital discharge or appointment?

You can request a scheduled transfer in advance. Confirmation depends on vehicle, crew, route, and timing availability; share your requirements with dispatch as early as possible.

4. Do you provide long-distance outstation ambulance transfers from Bangalore?

Outstation requests can be discussed with dispatch. Route, patient condition, crew arrangements, equipment, availability, and fare need to be assessed and confirmed before travel.

5. Is an EMT or paramedic present in the ambulance during transport?

Crew composition depends on the requested vehicle and availability. Confirm whether an EMT, paramedic, or nurse will accompany the patient and what care is included when booking.
