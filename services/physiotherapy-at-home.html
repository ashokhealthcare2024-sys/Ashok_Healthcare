What to Expect: Four Steps to Home Physiotherapy

01 · Clinical assessment

Review movement, strength, function, pain, and relevant medical or surgical precautions.

02 · Individual care plan

Agree goals and a therapy plan informed by assessment and the doctor's prescription where available.

03 · Guided home sessions

One-to-one sessions use appropriate exercises and portable tools as selected by the therapist.

04 · Progress review

Review mobility, function, and response to therapy; share updates with the physician where appropriate and with consent.

  1. Comprehensive assessment: A physiotherapist may assess range of motion (ROM), muscle strength, joint stability, function, and pain.
  2. Personalized plan: Exercises and pain-management approaches are tailored to recovery goals and any post-operative instructions.
  3. Home sessions: Session timing and frequency are agreed with the family and therapist based on clinical needs and availability.
  4. Progress tracking: Relevant measures and functional goals are reviewed over time; individual outcomes and recovery timelines vary.

Physiotherapy Equipment for Home Sessions

The therapist selects tools based on the assessment and treatment plan. Equipment use is not appropriate for every person; any electrical or other modality should be used under professional guidance.

TENS

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation may be considered for symptom management in selected cases.

IFT

Interferential therapy is used only when assessed as appropriate by the treating professional.

Ultrasound therapy

May be considered for some musculoskeletal conditions based on assessment and contraindications.

Rehabilitation aids

Goniometers, balance pads, therapy balls, mobilization belts, and resistance bands may support exercise and progress assessment.

Advanced items such as CPM machines or parallel bars may be available through Ashok Surgical, subject to stock, clinical suitability, and delivery arrangements. See medical equipment rental and sales.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long is each home physiotherapy session?

A standard session is generally planned for about 45 to 60 minutes. Duration depends on assessment, patient tolerance, the care plan, and therapist recommendation.

2. Do I need a doctor's prescription to start home physiotherapy?

A doctor's prescription and relevant discharge or imaging notes are helpful, especially after surgery or serious illness. For general mobility or musculoskeletal concerns, contact the team to discuss an initial assessment.

3. Are your home physiotherapists qualified?

Appointments are assigned to qualified physiotherapy professionals. Ask the care team about the assigned therapist's degree, registration, experience, and suitability for your condition when booking.

4. How many sessions will I need to recover?

The number of sessions varies with diagnosis, severity, goals, and response to therapy. Your physiotherapist can review progress and recommend a schedule; recovery timelines cannot be guaranteed.

5. What happens if my assigned therapist is unable to attend a session?

Contact the care team to discuss rescheduling or a suitably qualified replacement, subject to therapist availability and continuity-of-care requirements.
