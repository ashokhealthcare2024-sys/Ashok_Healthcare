Comprehensive Home Healthcare Services in Bangalore

Elderly Care & Assisted Living

Support to help senior citizens maintain dignity, safety, and independence at home.

  • Personal hygiene, bathing, dressing, and mobility support.
  • Medication reminders and blood pressure or glucose tracking as arranged.
  • Companionship and daily engagement for people living with cognitive conditions.
  • Feeding assistance aligned with the family’s and clinician’s guidance.

Professional Home Nursing Care

Registered clinical nurses provide skilled nursing support at home according to the prescribed care plan.

  • Post-operative wound dressing and infection-control practices.
  • IV care, catheter support, and Ryle’s Tube/PEG feeding as prescribed.
  • Tracheostomy and palliative nursing support.
  • 12-hour day/night and 24-hour care arrangements, subject to availability.

Home ICU Setup & Critical Care

Home equipment and bedside nursing support for patients who need a higher level of care.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How quickly can you set up an ICU at home in Yeshwanthpur or North Bangalore?

We aim to arrange critical-care equipment and nursing deployment within 3 to 4 hours for many requests. Our Yeshwanthpur hub serves areas including Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Mathikere, Hebbal, and Peenya. Timing depends on clinical assessment, location, staff, and equipment availability.

2. What is the difference between an elderly caregiver and a home nurse?

An elderly caregiver generally helps with personal hygiene, mobility, feeding, medication reminders, and companionship. A qualified home nurse provides clinical nursing care within their training and scope of practice, such as wound dressing, catheter care, IV care, or vital monitoring when prescribed.

3. Can we rent medical equipment like oxygen concentrators or ICU beds on a weekly basis?

Ashok Surgical offers flexible rental options, including daily, weekly, and monthly packages for available oxygen concentrators, hospital beds, monitors, and other equipment. Contact the team to confirm availability, rental terms, and suitability for the patient.

4. How do you verify the background and safety of your home healthcare staff?

The staff screening process includes clinical competency and credential evaluation, previous employment verification, and local police background checks.

5. What happens if an ICU machine displays an error code or malfunctions at night?

Contact the 24/7 technical support desk for guidance and service coordination. Technician dispatch and replacement equipment depend on the device, location, and availability. If the patient's condition is urgent, contact emergency services and follow the treating clinician's instructions.

6. Can your home nurses handle Ryle’s tube feeding, catheterization, and tracheostomy care?

Qualified nurses may provide tube-feeding support, catheter care, tracheostomy suctioning, IV care, and wound management when included in the doctor's care plan and within the assigned nurse's training and scope of practice.

7. Do your physiotherapists provide home visits for stroke and knee replacement rehabilitation?

Ashok Healthcare offers home physiotherapy and neuro-rehabilitation in Bengaluru, including areas such as Yeshwanthpur, Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, and Hebbal. A therapist can assess the patient and recommend an individualized program based on their condition and treating team's advice.

8. How do you coordinate with our primary hospital doctor during home recovery?

Care planning can be coordinated with the treating physician using discharge summaries, prescriptions, and relevant patient observations, subject to the patient's consent and the information shared by the family.

9. What are the payment options and pricing structures for home care and equipment rentals?

The team can discuss daily, weekly, or monthly arrangements for nursing shifts and equipment rentals. Contact Ashok Healthcare for current rates, accepted payment methods, availability, and any applicable fees before confirming a booking.

10. What is the minimum duration for hiring an elderly caretaker or home nurse in Bangalore?

Arrangements may include 12-hour day or night shifts and 24-hour care. Minimum duration, extensions, and availability depend on the service and should be confirmed with the care team.
