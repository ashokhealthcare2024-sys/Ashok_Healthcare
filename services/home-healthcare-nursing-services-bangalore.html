Detailed Clinical Offerings

1. Skilled Bedside Nursing Care

Qualified GNM and B.Sc Nursing professionals provide home nursing according to the patient's prescribed care plan and the nurse's scope of practice.

  • Post-operative recovery: Wound care, drain observation, and infection-control practices.
  • Chronic condition support: Routine observations, prescribed medication support, and diabetic foot care where included in the care plan.
  • Airway and tube care: Tracheostomy suctioning, stoma care, PEG feeding support, and oxygen equipment observation as prescribed.

2. Specialized Rehabilitation (Neuro & Ortho)

Home rehabilitation is planned around the patient's goals and recommendations from the treating clinician or therapist.

  • Stroke and paralysis care: Mobility exercises and active/passive range-of-motion practice as assessed.
  • Post-joint replacement: Rehabilitation after total knee or hip replacement, focused on safe movement and functional goals.
  • Pressure injury prevention: Repositioning guidance and wound care according to the clinical plan.

3. Home ICU Setup & Critical Care

Coordinate selected home ICU equipment and nursing support following a clinical assessment, subject to equipment and staff availability.

  • Equipment: Portable ventilators, multipara monitors, syringe/infusion pumps, and suction units.
  • Bedside nursing: Care is matched to the patient's needs, assigned professional's credentials, and treating-clinician directions.
  • Equipment monitoring, power backup, and technical support details should be confirmed for each setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between a Home Nurse and a Patient Caregiver?

A qualified GNM or B.Sc Nursing professional may provide clinical nursing care within their training and scope of practice, following the patient's care plan. A patient caregiver provides non-clinical assistance with daily activities such as personal hygiene, bathing, feeding, mobility, and companionship.

2. How quickly can medical equipment like hospital beds or oxygen concentrators be delivered?

Ashok Surgical coordinates delivery and setup for available equipment. Timing depends on inventory, location, and setup requirements. Contact the team for a current estimate.

3. How do you handle staff emergencies or nurse absences?

Contact the Yeshwanthpur care team promptly so they can assess staffing and arrange a suitable replacement if one is available. Replacement timing depends on location, clinical requirements, and staff availability; a specific response time cannot be guaranteed.

4. What qualifications and certifications do your home healthcare nurses hold?

Nursing staff include GNM and B.Sc Nursing professionals. Additional critical-care credentials and experience vary by nurse and assignment. Families can confirm the assigned professional's qualifications and suitability with the care team.

5. Can your home nurses coordinate directly with our primary hospital doctor?

Care planning can follow discharge instructions and the treating physician's directions. Patient observations may be documented and shared with the treating team with the patient's consent.

6. Do you offer trial sessions or short-term agreements for medical equipment rentals?

Daily, weekly, and monthly rental arrangements may be available for selected equipment. Contact Ashok Surgical to confirm current stock, pricing, rental terms, and suitability.

7. How do you ensure safety and hygiene for medical equipment delivered to our home?

Equipment is checked and prepared before dispatch according to the provider's handling procedures. Ask the team about cleaning, functional testing, packaging, and setup for the specific item you plan to rent or purchase.
