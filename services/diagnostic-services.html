Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential answers regarding home diagnostic tests, sample collection, reporting, and equipment.

How do I prepare for a fasting blood test at home?

For tests requiring fasting (such as Fasting Blood Sugar or Lipid Profile), do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 8 to 12 hours prior to sample collection. Our phlebotomist can visit early in the morning between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM.

How will I receive my diagnostic test reports?

Digital PDF reports signed by certified pathologists are sent directly to your WhatsApp and Email within 6 to 12 hours for routine tests. Specialized cultures or vitamin panels may take 24 to 48 hours.

Can I get an ECG done at home?

Yes. Our technician carries a compact, high-precision 12-lead digital ECG machine to perform cardiac screenings right at your bedside, with digital traces reviewed by a cardiologist.

Are there extra home collection charges?

Home sample collection charges are kept minimal and are frequently waived for bookings that meet minimum test value or standard health checkup package thresholds.

Are your diagnostic partner laboratories accredited?

Yes. All pathology samples are processed through trusted NABL-accredited and ISO-certified diagnostic laboratories in Bangalore to guarantee high analytical accuracy and reliability.
