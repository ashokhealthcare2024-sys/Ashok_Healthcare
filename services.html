FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our home healthcare, ICU setup, and medical equipment services.

Q1: Which areas in Bangalore do you cover for home healthcare and equipment delivery?

Our primary operations hub is situated in Yeshwanthpur. We offer 3-to-4-hour rapid delivery and nursing deployment across Yeshwanthpur, Rajajinagar, Malleshwaram, Hebbal, Mathikere, Peenya, Jalahalli, Basaveshwar Nagar, Mahalakshmi Layout, Sadashivanagar, Vidyaranyapura, Yelahanka, and Greater Bangalore.

Q2: How quickly can you set up a ventilator or Home ICU for a patient discharged today?

We can install a complete Home ICU setup—including a portable ventilator, multipara monitor, suction unit, and critical care nurse—within 3 to 4 hours of booking anywhere in North Bengaluru.

Q3: Can we rent medical equipment like oxygen concentrators or ICU beds on a weekly basis?

Yes. Through our equipment division, Ashok Surgical, we offer flexible daily, weekly, and monthly rental terms for oxygen concentrators, hospital beds, and cardiac monitors to suit your recovery budget.

Q4: Are your home nurses qualified to manage tracheostomy care and catheterization?

Yes. All our home nurses are qualified GNM or BSc Nurses trained in advanced clinical procedures, including tracheostomy suctioning, Foley catheter care, IV line management, and Ryle's tube feeding.

Q5: How do you handle medical equipment breakdowns during late hours?

We operate a dedicated 24/7 technical support desk. If a machine issues an alert or malfunctions at night, our local technicians in Bangalore are dispatched immediately with a replacement unit.
