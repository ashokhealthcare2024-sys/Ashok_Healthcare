Product Specifications & Usage Guidance

Respiratory & sleep-care equipment

  • Oxygen concentrators (5L/10L): Model specifications, flow range, oxygen concentration, alarms, and power requirements vary. Use oxygen only at the flow and duration prescribed; do not change settings without medical advice.
  • BiPAP & CPAP: Device features may include pressure adjustment, ramp functions, and humidification, depending on model. A qualified clinician must prescribe and configure therapy pressures.
  • Portable ventilators and cylinders: Device choice, settings, backup supply, and caregiver training require clinical assessment and a respiratory-care plan.
  • Nebulizers and suction machines: Select the device and accessories according to the clinician's instructions and the patient's needs.

Hospital beds & patient comfort

  • Manual Fowler beds: Hand-crank adjustment of back and/or knee sections, depending on configuration.
  • Electric ICU beds: Available functions vary by model; check weight capacity, dimensions, rail design, power, and caregiver requirements before ordering.
  • Alternating-pressure air mattresses: Mattress type and pump settings should be chosen with a clinician; repositioning and skin checks remain important parts of pressure-injury prevention.
  • Overbed tables: Ask the team to confirm dimensions and compatibility with the selected bed.

Critical-care monitoring & infusion support

  • Patient monitors: Available channels can include ECG, SpO₂, non-invasive blood pressure, respiration, and temperature; parameters depend on the model and configuration.
  • Infusion and syringe pumps: Use and programming are for trained clinical personnel under a prescription and care plan.
  • Defibrillators and crash-cart equipment: Availability and setup are confirmed case by case; devices require trained operators.

Mobility & rehabilitation equipment

  • Manual and motorized wheelchairs: Confirm seat width, patient weight capacity, transfer needs, terrain, and battery arrangements.
  • DVT compression pumps: Use only when prescribed and after checking suitability with the treating clinician.
  • CPM machines: May be considered for selected post-operative knee plans when approved by the surgeon or physiotherapist.
  • Walkers, canes, commodes, and shower chairs: Select for safe fit and use with clinician or therapist guidance where needed.

Equipment Hygiene, Inspection & Handover

Equipment is prepared for dispatch through cleaning and functional checks appropriate to the item. The process may include:

01 · Cleaning

Cleaning and disinfection of reusable surfaces using a method suitable for the device and manufacturer guidance.

02 · Function check

Inspection of visible components, controls, alarms, accessories, and power or battery functions as applicable.

03 · Calibration status

Calibration or performance checks are handled as applicable to the equipment type and service requirements.

04 · Safe handover

Where included in the booking, the team explains basic operation, known limitations, and whom to contact for support.

Patient-contact consumables, such as masks, nasal cannulas, tubing, and filters, are supplied according to the product, booking, and current availability. Ask which items are included, reusable, or single-use before the order is confirmed.

Home ICU Equipment Setup Options

Families can ask about combining equipment for a home-care setup. Configuration and timing depend on the patient's prescribed plan, home assessment, power and space requirements, stock, and nursing availability.

Basic monitored-care equipment

  • Electric or manual hospital bed, selected for the care plan.
  • Pressure-redistribution mattress when appropriate.
  • Patient monitor and suction apparatus if prescribed.
  • Infusion or syringe pump where required and managed by a qualified professional.

Respiratory-support equipment

  • Hospital bed and monitoring equipment based on assessment.
  • Ventilator or non-invasive ventilation device only as prescribed.
  • Oxygen supply and backup planning as advised by the treating team.
  • Ask separately about nursing support, setup, training, and emergency backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What medical equipment can I rent or buy from Ashok Healthcare in Bangalore?

Equipment options may include oxygen concentrators, BiPAP/CPAP machines, hospital beds, patient monitors, ventilators, infusion and syringe pumps, suction machines, air mattresses, wheelchairs, CPM machines, and mobility aids. Availability and rental or purchase terms are confirmed when you enquire.

2. What documentation is required to rent medical equipment?

Requirements depend on the equipment. The team may request identity and Bengaluru address verification and, for some items, a refundable security deposit. Confirm required documents, rental charges, and deposit/refund conditions before booking.

3. What should I do if rented equipment develops a fault at home?

Call the support line and describe the device, alert, and patient situation. The team can advise and arrange service or a replacement subject to location, device, and availability. If the patient is in immediate distress, contact emergency services or the treating clinician.

4. Will your team demonstrate how to use the equipment?

Where setup or demonstration is included, the team can explain basic operation and safety checks to the family or caregiver. Clinical settings and treatment parameters must be prescribed or confirmed by the treating clinician.

5. Can I upgrade equipment during the rental period?

Contact the team to discuss a different device if care needs change. An upgrade depends on clinical suitability, stock, delivery arrangements, and revised rental or purchase terms.
