Our Story & Founder’s Vision

Driven by Over a Decade of Hospital & Emergency Experience

“When a patient leaves the hospital, their recovery journey is only half complete. Families often face immense stress trying to manage post-discharge care, ICU machinery, and continuous nursing. Ashok Healthcare was created to help ease that anxiety by bringing hospital-standard care straight to your bedroom.”

Ashok Doddamani, Founder & CEO

Founded on April 1, 2023, Ashok Healthcare grew from hands-on nursing and emergency medical services. Having managed critical patient transfers and emergency hospital care, our founder recognized the need for reliable, clinically supervised home care in Bangalore.

Today, based in Yeshwanthpur, we work with patients, families, and treating clinicians to coordinate recovery support at home across North Bengaluru and other service areas.

About Our Organization

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where is Ashok Healthcare located, and which areas in Bangalore do you cover?

Our operations hub is at #28, Ground Floor, 4th Cross Rd, A.T. Street, Dr. Ambedkar Nagar, Yeshwanthpur, Bengaluru. Service areas include Yeshwanthpur, Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Mathikere, Hebbal, Peenya, Jalahalli, Yelahanka, Sadashivanagar, and other Greater Bengaluru locations, subject to availability.

2. What qualifications do your home healthcare nurses and caregivers possess?

Nursing staff include qualified GNM and B.Sc Nursing professionals. Clinical responsibilities depend on the individual’s credentials, experience, care plan, and scope of practice. Caregivers provide non-clinical assistance with personal hygiene, mobility, and daily activities.

3. How does Ashok Healthcare ensure safety for elderly patients living alone?

Staff screening includes credential and reference checks and local police verification. Supervisory follow-ups can be arranged to review care quality and safety in line with the care plan.

4. Does Ashok Healthcare coordinate with my hospital doctor after discharge?

Yes. Home-care planning can use the hospital discharge summary and prescriptions and can be coordinated with the treating physician. Patient observations may be shared with the treating team with the patient's consent.
