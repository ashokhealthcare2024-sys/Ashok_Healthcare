Home Nursing Charges in Bangalore 2026: 12-Hour vs. 24-Hour Cost Guide

Compare care levels, shift options, and monthly estimates to plan home healthcare around your family’s needs.

Finding reliable and affordable medical care for a loved one recovering from surgery, managing a chronic illness, or requiring geriatric assistance can be overwhelming. Understanding home nursing charges in Bangalore helps families compare options and plan for care without losing sight of the patient's clinical needs.

Pricing varies by caregiver qualification, shift schedule, clinical requirements, and duration. Whether you need an attendant for a few hours or round-the-clock nursing, this guide summarizes 2026 estimates and common factors that affect the total cost. The figures are for budgeting only; ask for a current written quote based on a clinical assessment.

Understanding Home Nursing Cost in Bangalore: Key Factors That Drive Pricing

The price of home nursing is not uniform. A patient recovering from a complex cardiac procedure may need a different level of skill and supervision than an older adult who needs help with mobility and daily routines.

Qualification and skill level

Attendant, ANM, GNM, B.Sc, and critical-care nursing roles have different training and scopes. Match duties to the caregiver's qualifications and the clinician's care plan.

Shift duration and schedule

Day, night, hourly visits, and 24-hour arrangements require different staffing patterns and affect daily and monthly rates.

Patient's medical needs

Post-stroke recovery, dementia support, palliative care, and ICU-level needs may require added skills or oversight.

Location and equipment

Travel, care availability, and separately supplied medical equipment can affect the final quotation.

Common caregiver qualification levels

  • Patient care attendants: Support with bathing, grooming, feeding, mobility, and routine daily activities.
  • ANM / GNM nurses: Trained for nursing duties within their qualifications, such as prescribed wound care, catheter care, injections, and monitoring.
  • B.Sc / critical-care nurses: May provide more advanced clinical support, including equipment-related care, when qualified and working under appropriate medical direction. For higher-acuity needs, review home ICU care options.

Discuss clinical duties with the treating doctor and the agency; attendants should not be expected to perform procedures beyond their training.

Home nursing charges per hour

For a short visit—such as an injection, dressing change, or catheter-related procedure when prescribed—an agency may offer a per-visit or hourly service.

  • Single visit of approximately 1–2 hours: ₹400–₹800 per procedure or visit.
  • Hourly care: May suit families that do not need a continuous shift. The indicative hourly rate card above ranges from ₹150 to ₹750 by caregiver category.

Nurse charges per month in Bangalore

Longer commitments may use a monthly package. The supplied estimates for 30 days or more are:

  • Attendant, 12 hours per day: ₹22,000–₹28,000.
  • Attendant, 24-hour live-in arrangement: ₹32,000–₹42,000.
  • Qualified nurse, 12 hours per day: ₹38,000–₹52,000.
  • Qualified nurse, 24-hour live-in arrangement: ₹65,000–₹90,000.

Monthly totals depend on staffing and included hours, so they may not equal a simple multiplication of a daily rate. Ask for an itemized quote and clarify relief coverage, rest periods, and additional charges.

Additional and Hidden Costs to Consider

Ask for a written estimate that identifies whether the following items are included or billed separately:

  1. Travel or conveyance: Some locations or shift schedules may involve additional travel charges.
  2. Medical consumables and equipment: Items such as syringes, catheters, diapers, gloves, dressing kits, suction machines, and oxygen concentrators may be separate costs. See the available medical equipment rental and sales options.
  3. Festival or holiday allowance: Ask whether the agreement includes a separate allowance for holiday shifts.
  4. Taxes and invoicing: Check whether the quotation includes applicable taxes and request an itemized invoice.

Financial Planning, Insurance, and Tax Considerations

Some insurance policies may cover home healthcare under specific domiciliary or post-hospitalization provisions. Coverage, prior authorization, eligible services, and required documents vary by policy; confirm directly with your insurer before relying on reimbursement.

Tax treatment depends on current law and individual circumstances. The source material mentions possible Section 80D deductions for qualifying senior-citizen medical expenses, but eligibility and limits can change and depend on the specific facts. Verify current rules with an official source or qualified tax professional rather than treating this guide as tax advice.

For recovery plans that include mobility work, discuss whether home physiotherapy is appropriate with the treating clinician. Care coordination should follow the patient's medical plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the average home nursing charges in Bangalore per day?

The estimates in this guide start around ₹700–₹1,000 for a 12-hour attendant shift and range from ₹1,200–₹2,500 for several qualified-nurse shift types. Critical-care and 24-hour rotating arrangements can cost more. Confirm rates based on the care plan.

2. How much does a 24-hour live-in nurse cost in Bangalore?

The guide estimates ₹1,200–₹1,600 per day for a live-in attendant and ₹2,200–₹3,200 for a qualified live-in nurse. Two-nurse rotating critical-care arrangements are estimated at ₹3,000–₹4,500 per day. Staffing and rest arrangements affect costs.

3. What is the difference between a patient attendant and a home nurse?

An attendant helps with daily living activities such as bathing, feeding, mobility, and hygiene. A qualified nurse can provide clinical care within their training and scope, according to the care plan.

4. Are 12-hour night nurse charges higher than day charges?

The supplied estimates show somewhat higher night rates. Exact charges depend on the care required, caregiver qualifications, and provider's current pricing.

5. Can I hire a home nurse on an hourly basis in Bangalore?

Per-visit services may be available for selected procedures. The guide estimates ₹400–₹800 for a 1–2-hour visit; confirm the procedure, prescription, availability, and quote with the provider.

6. Do monthly packages lower the overall home nursing cost in Bangalore?

A monthly package may offer different daily pricing from ad-hoc shifts, but savings are not guaranteed. Compare the total cost, staffing, coverage, and cancellation terms in writing.

7. What food and room arrangements are required for a 24-hour live-in nurse?

Discuss suitable hygienic accommodation, washroom access, meals, breaks, and rest periods with the caregiver and agency before care starts.

8. Does insurance cover home nursing charges in Bangalore?

It depends on the policy, medical indication, and insurer's requirements. Check coverage and authorization with your insurer before booking.

9. Are home nursing charges tax-deductible in India?

Eligibility depends on current tax rules and personal circumstances. The guide is not tax advice; verify applicable deductions and documentation with a qualified tax professional or official source.

10. How quickly can Ashok Healthcare deploy a nurse to my location in Bangalore?

Availability depends on location, caregiver availability, and the clinical assessment. Contact the care team to confirm a suitable start date.

11. What happens if the assigned home nurse goes on leave?

Ask whether the provider offers replacement staff, the expected response time, and how continuity is handled. Confirm any commitments in writing.

12. Are background checks conducted for home caregivers?

Ask the agency to describe its identity, address, reference, and professional-credential verification processes.

13. Which areas in Bangalore are covered by home care services?

Service availability varies by provider and staffing. Confirm coverage for your exact address, including any travel charges.

14. What is the cancellation policy for long-term home nursing contracts?

Cancellation terms vary. Review the notice period, billing for used days, refunds, and any minimum commitment in your written agreement.
