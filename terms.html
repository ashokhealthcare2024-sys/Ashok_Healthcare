1. Clinical Service Protocols

Ashok Home Healthcare Services delivers home nursing, elder care, post-operative physiotherapy, and medical equipment rental under the prescription and guidance of the patient's licensed treating physician or hospital discharge plan.

2. Medical Equipment Usage & Rental

All rental biomedical equipment (ventilators, BiPAP/CPAP machines, oxygen concentrators, motorized ICU beds, patient monitors) is sanitized, calibrated, and delivered with initial technician setup. Hirers agree to operate the equipment in accordance with manufacturer safety instructions and prevent unauthorized alteration.
