DETAILED PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS & USAGE GUIDE

Clinical performance standards, operational features, and medical applications.

🫁

1. Respiratory & Sleep Care

Home Oxygen Concentrators (5L / 10L):
Delivers continuous medical-grade oxygen (93% ± 3%) directly from room air. Built-in purity alarms and low-maintenance filters.
BiPAP & CPAP Machines:
Features auto-adjusting pressures, ramp settings, leak compensation, and integrated heated humidifiers to treat COPD, respiratory failure, and obstructive sleep apnea.
Portable ICU Ventilators:
Microprocessor-controlled ventilators with volume and pressure modes suitable for pediatric and adult ICU patients transitioning to home care.
🛏️

2. Hospital Beds & Patient Comfort

Manual Fowler Beds:
Dual-crank systems allowing manual elevation of head and knee panels for feeding, nursing, and basic recovery.
3-Function & 5-Function Electric ICU Beds:
Fully motorized beds operated via remote control. Enables precise height positioning, knee flex, back tilt, Trendelenburg, and Reverse Trendelenburg for high-dependency patients.
Ripple Air Mattresses (Anti-Bedsore):
Alternating air pressure cell design driven by a silent pump to prevent and manage Stage 1–4 bedsores in long-term bedridden individuals.
💓

3. Critical Care & Cardiac Support

Multipara Patient Monitors:
High-resolution digital displays for continuous monitoring of Heart Rate, ECG waveforms, SpO2, Non-Invasive Blood Pressure (NIBP), and Temperature.
Infusion & Syringe Pumps:
Precision delivery mechanisms ensuring accurate intravenous medication dosing for critical cardiac, oncological, or palliative patients.
♿

4. Mobility & Rehabilitation Equipment

Motorized Electric Wheelchairs:
Heavy-duty, rechargeable battery-powered mobility units with intuitive 360-degree joystick controls.
DVT Compression Pumps:
Sequential pneumatic compression sleeves that prevent blood clots in immobile or post-surgical lower limbs.
Continuous Passive Motion (CPM) Machines:
Motorized joint flexors utilized post-TKR (Total Knee Replacement) for rapid knee rehabilitation.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about medical equipment rental terms, documentation, fault replacement, and demonstrations.

Q1

What medical equipment can I rent or buy from Ashok Healthcare in Bangalore?

We provide rentals and sales for Oxygen Concentrators (5L/10L), BiPAP/CPAP machines, Hospital Beds (Manual, Semi-Electric, 5-Function Electric), Multipara Cardiac Monitors, Portable Ventilators, Syringe Pumps, DVT Pumps, Air Mattresses, Suction Machines, and Manual/Motorized Wheelchairs.

Q2

What documentation is required to rent medical equipment?

Rentals require minimal paperwork:

• A valid Government ID proof (Aadhaar Card / PAN / Driving License)
• Current local address proof in Bangalore
• A refundable security deposit (dependent on the equipment value)
Q3

What happens if an equipment machine develops a fault at home?

We offer a Rapid Swap Guarantee. If any rented device encounters a mechanical or electronic fault, our technician will visit within 2 to 4 hours to service the unit or swap it out with a replacement machine at no additional charge.

Q4

Will your team demonstrate how to use the equipment?

Yes. Our technician or accompanying clinical staff installs the equipment, tests all safety settings, configures default parameters, and provides a full hands-on demonstration to family members and caregivers before leaving.

Q5

Can I upgrade my equipment during the rental period?

Yes. If your patient's medical condition changes (e.g., transitioning from a manual bed to a 5-function electric ICU bed, or adding a BiPAP machine), you can request an upgrade at any time with adjusted rental terms.
