Four Steps to Home Physiotherapy Recovery

01 · Clinical Assessment

Evaluation of range of motion (ROM), muscle strength grading, postural alignment, pain mapping, and surgeon discharge instructions.

02 · Customized Care Plan

Establishing realistic short-term and long-term functional mobility milestones tailored to the patient’s home environment.

03 · One-on-One Guided Sessions

Dedicated 45–60 minute bedside sessions utilizing manual therapy, electrotherapy devices, and targeted corrective exercises.

04 · Milestone Reviews

Weekly progress reviews with objective measurement updates shared with family and treating orthopedic surgeons or neurologists.

Home Physiotherapy vs. Clinic Visits

Feature In-Clinic Physiotherapy Doorstep Home Physiotherapy
Travel Stress & Pain Traffic, car transfer pain, wheelchair handling, waiting rooms Zero travel — therapist arrives directly at bedside
Attention & Care Therapist often handles 3–4 patients simultaneously in gym 100% dedicated 1-on-1 therapist focus for entire session
Functional Training Generic clinic parallel bars and machines Real-life home practice (bed transfers, home stairs, bathroom safety)
Infection Risk Exposure to multiple sick patients in clinic/hospital environments Safest hygienic setting for elderly & post-operative patients
