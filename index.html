A Home Nurse (GNM/B.Sc Nursing) is a licensed medical professional who performs clinical procedures like IV drips, wound dressings, tracheostomy care, and catheterization. A Patient Caregiver (Attendant) provides non-clinical assistance with personal hygiene, bathing, feeding, mobility, and companionship.

All our nurses are qualified GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) or B.Sc Nursing graduates registered with the Nursing Council. Nurses assigned to ICU or critical care cases carry additional certifications in Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) or Critical Care Registered Nursing (CCRN), with hands-on training in managing mechanical ventilators, arterial lines, and emergency clinical protocols.
