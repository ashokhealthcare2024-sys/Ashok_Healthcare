Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I prepare for a fasting blood test at home?

For fasting tests such as FBS, Lipid Profile, and Insulin, an 8 to 12-hour overnight fast is required. You may drink plain water, but avoid tea, coffee, milk, breakfast, and smoking until the phlebotomist completes the draw.

2. How will I receive my diagnostic test reports?

Routine test reports (CBC, Sugar, LFT, KFT) are completed within 6 to 12 hours and delivered digitally via WhatsApp and secure email in PDF format. Specialized tests (Vitamins, Cultures) typically take 24 to 48 hours.

3. Can I get a 12-channel ECG done at home?

Yes. Our medical technicians bring portable hospital-grade 12-lead ECG machines directly to the bedside. You receive an immediate physical printout alongside a high-resolution PDF for instant doctor consultation.

4. Are there extra home collection charges?

Home collection charges are nominal and transparently communicated upon booking. Many of our health packages include free home collection across Yeshwanthpur, Malleshwaram, and surrounding North Bengaluru zones.

5. Are your diagnostic partner laboratories accredited?

Yes. Ashok Healthcare strictly routes all diagnostic samples through NABL and CAP-accredited pathology laboratories ensuring standard-of-care medical accuracy accepted by all major hospitals.
