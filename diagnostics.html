Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I prepare for a fasting blood test at home?

Maintain 8 to 12 hours of overnight fasting. Only drink plain water until the sample is collected.

2. How will I receive my diagnostic test reports?

Verified reports are delivered directly to your WhatsApp and email as soon as laboratory processing completes.

3. Can I get an ECG done at home?

Yes, our clinical technicians carry portable ECG equipment and deliver printed and digital strips immediately.

4. Are there extra home collection charges?

We offer affordable transparent packages with nominal home collection fees across Bengaluru.
