Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between BiPAP and CPAP?

CPAP delivers a single continuous pressure (mainly for Obstructive Sleep Apnea). BiPAP delivers dual pressures—higher pressure for inhalation and lower for exhalation—essential for COPD and respiratory failure.

2. Who sets the pressure parameters?

Our clinical technician calibrates the machine according to your physician's prescription during home installation.

3. Is the mask new?

Yes, we provide a brand new, factory-sealed mask (nasal or full-face) with every rental.
