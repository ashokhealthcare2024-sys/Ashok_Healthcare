Elder Care Services: What Families Should Consider

"A practical guide to understanding what to look for when arranging professional elder care and home nursing for senior family members."

Aging parents often value their independence above all else. However, age-related mobility decline, Parkinson's disease, dementia, diabetes, or arthritis can make routine daily activities challenging and hazardous without assistance.

Key Questions Every Family Must Ask Before Hiring Care

  • Are background checks verified? At Ashok Healthcare, every caretaker undergoes strict Aadhaar identity verification, address authentication, and police verification.
  • Is there clinical backup? Unlike unorganized maid agencies, Ashok Healthcare provides nursing supervisors and emergency doctor escalations.
  • What shift model works best? 12-hour day shifts are ideal for seniors who live with working adult children, while 24-hour live-in attendants provide round-the-clock peace of mind for seniors living alone.
